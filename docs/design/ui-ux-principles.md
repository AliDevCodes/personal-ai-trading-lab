# UI/UX Principles

- Design for calm, selective decision support rather than high-frequency stimulation.
- Make market data, strategy outputs, deterministic risk state, AI commentary, and paper-trading state visibly distinct.
- Show the rationale and uncertainty behind a signal; do not present AI output as certainty or execution authority.
- Prioritize risk visibility, predefined-risk context, and journal reflection.
- Reduce over-analysis and strategy tweaking through focused views and evidence-oriented history.
- Optimize first for the 4H, 1H, and 15M workflow; detailed interaction design is TBD.
